Saba Karimi

I study how AI agents reason about images, especially when the evidence supports more than one answer.

I'm a PhD student in Machine Learning at Georgia Tech, with a minor in Materials Science. I work on agentic AI and probabilistic reasoning with foundation models, and on making scientific image analysis more reliable.

Before that I earned an M.Sc. in Computer Science (Machine Learning) at Georgia Tech and a B.Sc. in Materials Engineering at Sharif University of Technology. Microscopy brought me to computer vision.

Guiding question

How can an agent keep several readings of an image in play, weigh them, and act well before it knows which one is right?

Interests agentic AI · visual reasoning · uncertainty quantification · foundation segmentation models · scientific imaging
