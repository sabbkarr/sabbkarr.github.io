Saba Karimi

How can AI agents reason about what they see when an image supports more than one answer?

    I'm a PhD student in Machine Learning at Georgia Tech, with a minor in Materials Science. I received my M.Sc. in Computer Science from Georgia Tech and my B.Sc. in Materials Engineering from Sharif University of Technology.

    I study agentic AI and probabilistic reasoning with foundation models, especially for ambiguous visual and scientific images.

    Research interests: agentic AI, visual reasoning, uncertainty quantification, foundation segmentation models, scientific imaging.
