Bio

About me

I'm a PhD student in Machine Learning at Georgia Tech, minoring in Materials Science. My research is on agentic AI, probabilistic reasoning and foundation models. I develop uncertainty-aware reasoning frameworks, build ML pipelines that scale, and design evaluations for multimodal tasks that don't have a single correct output.

I trained as a materials engineer at Sharif University of Technology. At Georgia Tech I began by automating microscopy image analysis for cellulose nanocrystals, which led me to the broader question of how models reason about images, and to an M.Sc. in Computer Science focused on machine learning.

Outside research, I spend time with friends, write, and bake.
